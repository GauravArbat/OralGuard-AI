Overview

Aphthous ulcers (canker sores) are the most common type of oral ulceration. They are typically round or oval, with a yellowish-gray pseudomembranous center and an erythematous halo. Minor aphthous ulcers are less than 1 cm in diameter and heal spontaneously within 7-14 days without scarring.

Recommendation

Routine Follow-up
  • Clinical evaluation recommended as routine follow-up.
  • Reassess if the ulcer does not heal within 2 weeks.
  • Symptomatic management: topical analgesic/antiseptic mouthwash.
  • Avoid spicy, acidic, or abrasive foods until mucosal healing.
